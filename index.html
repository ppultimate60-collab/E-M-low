ED Flow V3

This version adds patient-linked investigations and automated clinical decision support.

  • ABG/VBG image + structured values
  • ECG upload + note
  • RBS and ketone tracking
  • Automatic scores and safety alerts
  • Automated treatment-support suggestions
  • Patient-specific investigation timeline
  • Printable report
Clinical safety: This is a prototype. Treatment suggestions are decision support, not autonomous prescribing. Verify all results, doses, contraindications and local protocols. Do not use unvalidated output as the sole basis for patient care.
